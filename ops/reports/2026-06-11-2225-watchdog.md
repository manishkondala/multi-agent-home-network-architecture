## Addendum: jsms_worker-au investigation & resolution (CTO, 22:30 EDT)

**Root cause found.** The three legacy containers were stopped **manually** in an interactive
SSH session from 192.168.1.159 (the owner's Mac) at 01:33–01:40 UTC (21:33 EDT). The Pi's
shell history shows a high-CPU investigation (`ps aux | grep highcpu`, `htop`) followed by
`docker stop` on three container IDs. This was **46 minutes before** this session's deploy
(homepage recreated 02:20 UTC) — the deploy did not touch them. The legacy containers also
carry no compose labels, so `--remove-orphans` could never match them.

**Action taken:** `jsms_worker-au` restarted at 02:26 UTC per standing order; verified stable
(CPU 0.03%, load avg 0.55). `happy_shannon` and `adoring_jones` left stopped pending owner
decision — if the owner stopped all three deliberately (high-CPU culprit?), say so and we'll
stop the worker again and amend the standing order.

**Also fixed:** corrected pihole metric names in `.claude/agents/watchdog.md`
(`pihole_ads_blocked_today` / `pihole_dns_queries_today`).
